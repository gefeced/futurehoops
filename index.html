Advanced Mode

Advanced Mode unlocks step controls, deeper play designer tools, and the Simulation Lab.

It is optional and intended for power users. Normal use does not require it.

How FutureHoops Works

Profile Lab is your player roster workspace. Use it to create or select athletes, set positions and archetypes, and tune ratings, confidence, and fatigue. The lineup area shows which players are active for play design and game scenarios.

Play Designer is for building plays step-by-step. Place offense and defense, draw routes and passes, set the ball holder, and preview timing. Steps always exist; Advanced Mode adds step controls, context details, and extra tools. Plays are saved locally so you can return and iterate later.
